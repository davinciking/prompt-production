### Slide 7: Sizing & Silhouette Cheat Sheet: The Tunnel Blueprint
* **Header:** The 2026 Tunnel Silhouette Rules
* **Rule 01: The 1:1.6 Cropped Torso Ratio**
  * Never let an overshirt or hoodie pass below your mid-zipper. Look for garment lengths between 24.5” and 26.5” (depending on height) to extend leg lines.
* **Rule 02: Drop-Shoulder Room for Broad Shoulders**
  * Avoid set-in sleeves. Kimono collars, raglan sleeves, and drop shoulders prevent chest-bunching while giving a relaxed drape over heavyweight tees.
* **Rule 03: The 9.5-Inch Hem Pool Rule**
  * Narrow leg openings are retired. Wide-leg pleated trousers or heavyweight canvas chinos must have a minimum 9.5-inch to 10.5-inch leg opening to cleanly pool over low-profile sneakers (Converse SHAI 001, Nike Book 1, vintage retro low-tops).
* **Rule 04: Two Heavyweight Layers > Four Thin Layers**
  * A 300gsm boxy mock-neck tee paired under a 400gsm unlined kimono overshirt creates architectural structure without bulk.
