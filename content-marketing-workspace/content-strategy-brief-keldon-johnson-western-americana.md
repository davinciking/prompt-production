## 5. High-Save Cheat Sheet Architecture (Carousel & Script Core)

To exceed the >3.5% save rate threshold, the content must provide concrete styling rules:

1. **The 8.5–9.5" Leg Opening Metric (Eliminating "Cowboy Muffin Top"):**
   * *The Problem:* Tapered or skinny denim (<7.5" hem) clings to the boot shaft, creating an awkward bulging ring ("shaft bulge" or cowboy muffin top).
   * *The Rule:* Pair Western boots exclusively with **8.5" to 9.5" leg openings** (straight leg, relaxed taper, or gentle bootcut selvedge). The denim drops straight down the shin and settles with a single clean stacked break right at the boot vamp.
2. **The "One-Piece Western" Golden Rule:**
   * Never wear the hat, the fringe jacket, the western shirt, and the boots simultaneously unless you are competing in a sanctioned rodeo.
   * Anchor your outfit with **one statement Western hero piece** (e.g., Lucchese roper boots OR a pearl-snap sawtooth shirt) and balance the rest with minimalist streetwear: a boxy 300 GSM heavyweight tee, double-knee carpenter pants, or an unlined chore coat.
3. **The Texture & Finish Hierarchy:**
   * *Avoid:* Ultra-glossy, lacquered exotic patent leathers that look like corporate boardroom kitsch.
   * *Embrace:* Matte steerhide, waxed flesh, roughout suede, and natural vegetable-tanned leathers that develop patina, creasing, and character with age.
4. **Proportion Math (Cropped Boxy Torso):**
   * Outerwear length should stop at or 1 inch above the beltline. High-rise trousers paired with cropped jackets create a 1/3 to 2/3 visual ratio that makes the wearer look taller and gives cowboy boots grounded presence.
