### Slide 2 — The 2015 Relic vs. The Modern Column
* **Headline:** **RAW DENIM DIDN'T DIE. THE 6-INCH HEM DID.**
* **Body Copy:**
  * **2015 Playbook:** Painted-on calves (`A.P.C. Petit Standard`, `Nudie`, `Saint Laurent D02`) with `6.0"–7.0"` hems, tight 2-inch cuffs, and aggressive knee honeycombs.
  * **Today's Standard:** Relaxed, straight, and wide cuts (`8.5"–10.0"` leg openings) that let the fabric hang like an architectural column.
  * **Ace's Take:** *"If your denim is clinging to your calves like compression gear, the silhouette is working against you."*
* **Visual Prompt:** Split-screen architectural diagram comparing a pinched 6.8" tapered leg silhouette (crossed out in muted crimson) vs. a clean 9.5" straight-drape selvedge column breaking over a chunky black loafer.

### Slide 3 — The Main Stage Catalyst (And The Price Trap)
* **Headline:** **THE KENDRICK EFFECT: DRAPE WITHOUT THE $1,200 TAX**
* **Body Copy:**
  * When **Kendrick Lamar** stepped onto the **2025 Super Bowl LIX Halftime Stage** in Hedi Slimane-designed **women's Celine flared surf jeans (~$1,200)** ([Observer](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiZkFVX3lxTE1DNmhGazFDc3NpODdWcHlHT00tSjZKRENpd2ExUllBaDJWWDRycHhWY2hQX0V4a2dJZEJNM1NnVkU1ejlnNXhXYXlWR1BRc3VHOW5jXzZTTmlXVzhiT3hoTjQ0cndBZw?oc=5)), mainstream menswear finally woke up to wide-leg denim geometry.
  * **The Trap:** Paying four figures for lightweight designer-logo wash denim when Kojima shuttle-loom mills build heavier, longer-lasting fabric for a fraction of the price.
  * **Rule:** *Clout is rented; quality is owned.*
* **Visual Prompt:** Minimalist editorial comparison card: Left side shows a `$1,200` luxury price tag silhouette; Right side shows a macro swatch of deep rope-dyed Japanese shuttle-loom selvedge with `17oz • ~$240 USD` stamped in crisp archival type.

### Slide 4 — Tokyo's Twin Denim Corridors (Insider Lore)
* **Headline:** **WHERE THE HOLDERS SHOP IN TOKYO**
* **Body Copy:**
  * **1. Ueno Ameyoko (Under the JR Tracks — Est. 1949):** **Hinoya** & **Americaya** stock wall-to-wall Kojima selvedge (`Oni Denim`, `Momotaro`, `Burgus Plus`, `Sugar Cane`) at domestic Japan prices (**¥33,000–¥41,800 / ~$220–$280 USD**) with complimentary vintage Union Special chain-stitch hemming ([Time Out Tokyo](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMid0FVX3lxTFBhaXY1OEhOdkdZMmZHRzlCM0FGakM4dmNqaVBRc0hWTjg1QnhBbWtzTHpUT2U1WVRZcDBkWHYzUTZKamtveno4Q1JpdmxibU5OckJDZFBaUmJxLVA5Z2lFek9CNFFLSUw1Q3M5bC1aMjZUVUZ0T1Fz?oc=5)).
  * **2. Ebisu & Shibuya (Artisanal Boutiques):** **Kapital Ebisu** & **Kapital Legs** push textile art—`12oz–14oz` **Century Denim No. 5S** woven with persimmon-tannin *Kakishibu* dye and continuous indigo sashiko stitching (**¥28,000–¥45,000** in Japan / **$350–$450** US).
* **Visual Prompt:** Clean two-column Tokyo transit-inspired map card contrasting **UENO AMEYOKO (Heritage Value)** and **EBISU / SHIBUYA (Artisanal Craft)** with macro fabric cutouts of Oni slub denim and Kapital Kakishibu sashiko.

### Slide 5 — The Textile Spec: Why 15oz–17oz Wins Wide Cuts
* **Headline:** **HEAVYWEIGHT SPEC: WHY OZ WEIGHT MATTERS**
* **Body Copy:**
  * **Flimsy Stretch (10oz–12oz + Elastane):** Collapses inward at the ankle, bags at the knee, and looks sloppy over structured leather shoes.
  * **Vintage Toyoda Shuttle-Loom (15oz–19oz Raw):** Woven at low tension in Kojima, Okayama for slubby vertical texture and structural body ([GQ](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiVEFVX3lxTE1lclIzWUVMa2NCU2NlZHpydWpGTjJ0X185c1FuTlFneTY0UHBVZmxNSmtLeGZRMlloTGZSTFh0ZDItaXhLdE5ELW9Xa0djcmlYVFdSTw?oc=5)).
  * **Result:** Holds a crisp, column-like architectural fall and stacks intentionally over a loafer vamp.
* **Visual Prompt:** 85mm macro side-by-side fabric weave comparison: flat synthetic stretch denim vs. bumpy, hairy, rope-dyed 17oz Japanese selvedge with red-ticker selvedge edge.
