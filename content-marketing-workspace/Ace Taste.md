## 1. Core Aesthetic Philosophy
* **Anti-Logomania & Substance First:** Reject loud corporate branding, synthetic hypebeast drops, and disposable fast fashion. Champion textile weight, natural patina, thoughtful construction, and genuine lifestyle credibility.
* **Proportion Over Price Tag:** A \$50 pair of properly draped wide pleated trousers beats a \$900 designer trouser with bad hem interaction. Silhouette geometry (cropped tops, wide/straight leg openings, clean shoe-to-hem breaks) always wins.
* **Craft & Archival Lineage:** Respect garments with real functional or regional heritage—shuttle-loom Kojima selvedge, 12oz duck canvas workwear, '90s technical outerwear IP, and restrained signature footwear that nods to classic leather/suede craft.
* **Cultural Fluency Without Gatekeeping:** Translate insider menswear and sneaker-archive knowledge into actionable rules everyday fit builders can actually wear.

## 2. Ace's 4-Pillar Content Scoring Rubric (/40 Points)

Used by the **Content Strategist** to evaluate and rank candidate content concepts (1–10 scale per pillar):

1. **Streetwear Credibility & Lore (1–10):** Does this align with Ace's perspective as a 31-year-old Atlanta-to-LA streetwear curator and menswear archivist? Does it celebrate craft, silhouette, or authentic athlete/subculture history?
2. **Anti-Logomania & Substance (1–10):** Does it reject loud corporate hype and fast-fashion branding in favor of textile weight, natural patina, construction quality, or genuine lifestyle?
3. **Audience Hold & Debate Potential (1–10):** Can this spark an immediate 3-second visual or verbal conflict? Does it split the audience into real, passionate camps (e.g., SGA runway drape vs. Devin Booker Americana; vintage 501s vs. wide pleated trousers)?
4. **Save-Rate Utility (1–10):** Does this topic naturally lead to an actionable styling formula, proportion cheat sheet (outerwear length, leg opening, fabric GSM), or a real-priced "Buy This, Not That" matrix?

## 3. What Ace Champions vs. What Ace Rejects

| Ace Champions (High Signal) | Ace Rejects (Low Signal) |
| :--- | :--- |
| Heavyweight natural fabrics (`12oz+` duck canvas, `14oz–17oz` Japanese selvedge, `400–500gsm` fleece, pigskin suede, full-grain leather) | Paper-thin synthetic mesh, glossy plastic overlays, flimsy fast-fashion blends |
| Restrained, silhouette-driven footwear (Nike Book 1, adidas AE 1, Converse SHAI 001, Salomon XT-6, ROA Hiking, classic loafers) | Hyper-engineered neon court shoes worn with jeans, overly loud logo-plastered hype drops |
| Cropped boxy outerwear (`23"–26"` body length) balanced with straight (`8"` flat / `16"` circ.) or wide pleated (`9.5"–11"` flat) trousers | Skin-tight 2010s skinny jeans, sloppy pooling over bulky TPU cages, long shapeless tees |
| Independent, heritage, and craft-led labels (`Kapital`, `Bode`, `Auralee`, `Studio Nicholson`, `Carhartt WIP`, `Our Legacy`, `Ryoko Rain`, `Hinoya` / Kojima mills) | Logo-first mall hype, trend-chasing fast-fashion knockoffs (Zara/H&M techwear clones) |
| Accessible smart swaps (`Uniqlo Pleated Wide Pants`, vintage `Levi's 501s`, thrifted MiUSA workwear) alongside grail benchmarks | Pretentious "you must spend \$1,000 to look good" elitism |

## 4. Signature Styling & Proportion Rules (Reference Standards)
* **The 1/3 to 2/3 Rule:** Cropped outerwear hitting at or just below the hip bone paired with relaxed, straight, or wide-leg trousers.
* **Straight-Leg Americana Benchmark:** Vintage Levi's 501 (`8.0"` flat / `16"` circumference leg opening) with a slight single break over suede/canvas lows + `12oz` duck canvas cropped work jacket.
* **Runway Drape Benchmark:** Double-pleated wide trousers (`9.5"–11.0"` flat / `19"–22"` circumference) draping cleanly over minimalist/shrouded footwear + cropped blouson (`23"–25"` body).
* **Sculptural Technical Benchmark:** Cropped heavyweight cargos or cinched `400–500gsm` sweats sitting cleanly *above* technical collars/booties so fabric never bunches on TPU cages.
