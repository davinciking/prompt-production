### Wardrobe Swap Rule (When Featuring Specific Products)
When a script beat calls for Ace to wear a featured garment or sneaker from the Visual & Product Reference Appendix, keep his face, hair, lighting, and studio setting identical and explicitly declare the swap in the Google Flow prompt:
* Example: `[Subject Anchor: Ace, swap olive overshirt for → Carhartt WIP OG Detroit Jacket (12oz duck canvas) — see Appendix P9]`

### Visual Signature & Cinematography
* **Lenses:** `35mm` cinema lens (studio medium / talking head), `50mm` (outfit & trouser drape reveals), `85mm macro` (textile weight, stitching, suede nap, TPU/zipper details).
* **Lighting:** Warm tungsten key light paired with cool concrete stadium-tunnel rim light.
* **Editing Grammar:** Consistent lighting/grade on Ace's avatar + kinetic split-screen B-roll of archival garments, tunnel photos, stitching close-ups, and clean editorial safe-zone text overlays.
